04 · what it does

Small app. Big ears.

01

Talk for as long as you need.

Long recordings spool to a temporary file and are transcribed in chunks, so memory stays flat. The audio is deleted when the session ends.

02

clipboard→active window→restored

Lands where you were typing.

Eve copies, pastes into the active window, and can put your previous clipboard back afterwards.

03

so um basically ship itShip it.

Raw, or tidied up.

Keep exactly what you said, or use Clean Prompt, Codex Prompt, Message Rewrite, or Command Mode for local, rule-based cleanup.

Hotwords help Eve spell names, acronyms, and product terms the way you do.

05

large-v3-turbolarge-v3small

Faster-Whisper, your pick.

large-v3-turbo by default, large-v3 for maximum accuracy, or small for lighter hardware. Switching never interrupts the current model.

06

cpugpu · cuda pack

CPU first. GPU optional.

Works out of the box on the CPU. An optional NVIDIA CUDA pack, verified by hash, speeds things up on supported GPUs.

05 · privacy

Your voice never leaves your computer. No account. No cloud. No telemetry.

recognition
Speech runs in a service bundled with the app. Audio and transcripts are never uploaded.
history
History and Insights live in a SQLite file on your machine. Long-dictation audio is temporary.
network
Only for downloads: the installer payload, model weights on first use, and the optional GPU pack.
telemetry
None. No account, no analytics. Copied diagnostics exclude logs, paths, and transcript text.
